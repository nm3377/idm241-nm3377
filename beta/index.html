IDM 241: Beta Description

Youtube

Object: Dislike Button

Location

The dislike button can be found almost directly in the center of the page horizontally, on the right side of the bottom of the video player. It is to the right of the like button, and to the left of the share button. By default, it has a bubble behind it and the outline of a thumbs down shape.

SOMETHING OF NOTE: THE LIKE COUNTER DOES NOT GO DOWN, AS DISLIKES ARE NOT PUBLIC.

Triggers

  • Hover ON
  • Hover OFF
  • Click ON
  • Click OFF

Rules

Hover ON

  • Cursor changes from arrow to pointer
  • Dialogue box fades in over the course of 0.5 seconds underneath the thumbs down icon reading "I Dislike This"
  • Backdrop behind the thumbs down icon goes from dark gray to light gray over the course of 0.5 seconds

Hover OFF

  • Cursor changes from pointer to arrow
  • Dialogue box fades out over the course of 0.3 seconds
  • Backdrop returns from light gray to dark gray over the course of 0.3 seconds

Click ON

  • Thumbs down icon switches from outline to filled with white over the course of 0.3 seconds
  • Thumbs down icon increases in size by 20% over the course of 0.5 seconds
  • Background flashes from light gray to a lighter gray over the course of 0.1 seconds
  • Monochrome confetti effect over the course of 0.5 seconds
  • Video is marked as disliked within the database

Click OFF

  • Thumbs down icon reverts back to outlined state over the course of 0.3 seconds
  • Thumbs down icon reverts back to outlined state over the course of 0.5 seconds
  • Background flashes from light gray to a lighter gray over the course of 0.1 seconds
  • Thumbs down icon increases in size by 10% over the course of 0.5 seconds
  • Video is removed from dislike section in database

Loops and Modes

Loops

N/A

Modes

After a "Click ON", the dislike button will enter a new mode, resulting in different text appearing in the dialogue box during a "Hover ON" than in the default state, reading "Undo Dislike" instead of "I Dislike This"

Object: More Options Button

Location

The more options button can be found below the bottom right edge of the video player. It is to the right of the bookmark button. By default, it has a bubble behind it and 3 hollow dots inside of the bubble.

Triggers

  • Hover ON
  • Hover OFF
  • Click ON
  • Click OFF

Rules

Hover ON

  • Cursor changes from arrow to pointer
  • Dialogue box fades in over the course of 0.5 seconds underneath the thumbs up icon reading "More Options"
  • Backdrop behind the thumbs down icon goes from dark gray to light gray over the course of 0.5 seconds

Hover OFF

  • Cursor changes from pointer to arrow
  • Dialogue box fades out over the course of 0.3 seconds
  • Backdrop returns from light gray to dark gray over the course of 0.3 seconds

Click ON

  • Three hollow dots icon switches from outline to filled with white over the course of 0.3 seconds
  • Three dots icon increases in size by 20% over the course of 0.5 seconds
  • Background flashes from light gray to a lighter gray over the course of 0.1 seconds
  • Dropdown menu with options "Download" and "Report" appear beneath the three dots icon
  • New button state is entered that changes how dropdown menu behaves

Click OFF

  • Three dots icon reverts back to outlined state over the course of 0.3 seconds
  • Three dots icon increases in size by 10% over the course of 0.5 seconds
  • Background flashes from light gray to a lighter gray over the course of 0.1 seconds
  • Dropdown menu with options "Download" and "Report" disappears
  • New button state is left and dropdown menu behavior is reverted
